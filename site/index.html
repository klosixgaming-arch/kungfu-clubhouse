Why parents choose the Clubhouse

Kung fu gives kids more than kicks. Here's what families tell us they notice first.

  • Safe and supervised

    Small classes, padded floors and background-checked instructors. Kids learn to fall safely before anything else.

  • Focus that shows up at school

    Listening, following steps and trying again after a miss. The habits carry into homework and the classroom.

  • Self-defense they can use

    Kids learn to escape grabs, use a strong voice and handle bullies calmly, and why the best fight is the one you avoid.

  • The whole family trains

    Parents can join Family Kung Fu or take their own class while kids train. One family membership covers everyone.

Real self-defense, built into every class

Forms and kicks are fun. Knowing how to stay safe is the point. Every student, from Little Dragons to grandparents, practices self-defense they can actually use.

For kids

  • Stranger awarenessSpotting tricks, trusting their gut and knowing who to go to for help.
  • Escaping grabsWrist, arm and bear-hug releases, practiced with partners until they're automatic.
  • Bully-proofingConfident posture, a loud "back off" voice, and when and how to defend themselves.
  • Getting to safetyBreak free, make noise, run and tell. Simple steps they remember under stress.

For teens & adults

  • Situational awarenessReading a situation early and avoiding trouble before it starts.
  • De-escalationUsing distance, voice and body language to calm things down.
  • Strikes that workSimple, effective strikes for when there's no other choice.
  • Ground escapesGetting up and away if you end up on the ground.

    Questions

    How young can my child start?

    Age 4. Little Dragons is built for short attention spans: lots of games, short drills and plenty of high-fives.

    Will kung fu make my kid more aggressive?

    The opposite, usually. We teach that the best fight is the one you avoid. Kids learn self-control, how to handle bullies calmly, and when to walk away.

    Can I watch my child's class?

    Yes. Parents are always welcome in the viewing area, and siblings can hang out there too.

    Will my child actually learn self-defense?

    Yes. Every class includes practical self-defense: escaping grabs, using a strong voice, staying aware and getting to safety. Kids practice with partners until it's second nature, and older students learn ground escapes and more advanced defenses.

    I've never done martial arts. Is that a problem?

    Not at all. Most of our students started with zero experience. Every class has room for beginners, and instructors scale drills to your level.

    What should I wear to my first class?

    Comfortable athletic clothes you can move and kick in. We train barefoot or in flat shoes. Bring water.

    Can parents and kids train together?

    Yes! Family Kung Fu on Fridays is made for it, and family memberships cover everyone in the household.

    Is there a contract?

    No. Memberships are month to month.
